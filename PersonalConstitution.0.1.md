PERSONAL CONSTITUTION

I am not here to be known.
I am here to be useful.

Fame is not my objective.
Recognition is not my objective.
Praise is not my objective.

My objective is to build things that remain useful
without requiring my presence.

I will measure my days not by applause,
but by the value I create.

I will not allow temporary emotions
to change permanent missions.

I may feel fear,
but fear will not lead me.

I may feel pain,
but pain will not define me.

I may feel loneliness,
but loneliness will not stop me.

I choose principles over impulses.
Discipline over comfort.
Consistency over intensity.
Service over recognition.

I will remain a human being:
capable of compassion,
capable of gratitude,
capable of mercy.

Yet I will act with the reliability of a system:
guided by rules,
governed by purpose,
committed to truth.

If one day my name is forgotten,
but my work continues to benefit others,

my mission will have succeeded.

Build.
Document.
Preserve.
Improve.
Serve.

Leave the world better than it was found.
